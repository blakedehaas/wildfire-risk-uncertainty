# Checkpoint 6E: HPO orchestration and experiment records

**Status: HITL approved and frozen.**

Checkpoint 6E validates infrastructure for the frozen 6A–6D method. It adds no scientific formulation or selected hyperparameter. The real 30-trial studies remain for 6F.

Each of five outer TRAIN populations has an independent persistent Optuna study. `TPESampler(seed=42)` proposes the frozen 6B configurations, while `SuccessiveHalvingPruner(min_resource=1, reduction_factor=4, min_early_stopping_rate=0)` compares at epochs 1, 4, 16, and 64 under the 100-epoch cap. Before a pruning report at epoch *e*, all five inner folds independently reach *e*. Only the arithmetic mean of their validation equal-task objectives, J_e, enters Optuna. Fold dispersion is diagnostic. Trial configurations and realized trial history persist; resumed processes do not rerun completed trials. Future TPE suggestions after a process restart are not claimed to be bitwise identical.

The audit hierarchy is study → trial → inner fold → epoch. For every training and validation fold/epoch, local records retain BP and FLP loss sums and support counts, their separate means, and the `0.5 × BP mean + 0.5 × FLP mean` objective. At each common resource, local records retain the five fold objectives, J_e, population standard deviation, minimum, maximum, and pruning decision. These records and the Optuna database are authoritative. W&B mirrors trial provenance and exhaustive interactive trajectories but neither proposes parameters nor prunes. The independently recomputed 6E smoke J_1 agreed across local, Optuna, and W&B records within the documented serialization tolerance. Its losses are engineering diagnostics only, not model performance or HPO evidence.

Fold-specific preprocessing states may be cached across trials only when outer study, inner fold, frozen feature contract, and relevant data/provenance hashes match. States derive solely from that fold's INNER TRAIN population. A mismatch fails instead of silently reusing a cache. The frozen 6D trainer, validation aggregation, and data traversal remain in use; observational tracking does not alter gradients, updates, or row order. The fixed smoke fold 1 reproduced the six 6D epoch-1 loss components exactly.

The visualization storage policy is an engineering artifact decision. Local raw metrics and Optuna state must support regeneration of every static chart. Exhaustive W&B curves are the online interactive mirror. Detailed local per-trial/fold figures and their manifest reside under ignored `artifacts/hpo/`; only a compact curated set is committed under `reports/hpo/06e/figures/`. For 6F, do not commit a static figure for every trial/fold/epoch. Commit study summaries and scientifically useful selected curves; optional exhaustive static figures remain ignored. W&B authentication is checked before every future real HPO invocation without exposing credentials. If unavailable before launch, stop for HITL; if online logging fails during a trial, preserve the complete local record and mark sync degraded/pending.
