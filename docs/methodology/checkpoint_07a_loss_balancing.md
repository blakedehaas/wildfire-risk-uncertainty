## Mathematical diagnostics and objective

For task `t`, let `C_t` be summed target cross entropy over its valid support `N_t`, and let `H_t` be summed target entropy on the same support. We report excess log loss `KL_t = (C_t − H_t) / N_t` in nats. The identity follows directly from the definition of cross entropy and KL divergence. Bernoulli entropy uses `−p log p − (1−p) log(1−p)`; conditional six-class entropy uses `−Σ q_k log q_k` only where FLP is valid. `torch.special.xlogy` handles zero probabilities without `0 log 0` NaNs. BP and FLP losses are never pooled across their different supports. Small negative roundoff below `1e−6` is clipped to zero; larger negative results fail.

The static training objective is `L(α) = α L_BP + (1−α) L_FLP`, with `0 < α < 1`. The existing `α=0.5` arithmetic and code path are retained exactly. FLP minibatch gradients use the frozen global ratio `N_BP / N_FLP`, so the row-weighted epoch contribution follows the same support-normalized objective. This is a project-specific choice of static weights, not a claim that a gradient-balanced weight is optimal.

For each inner TRAIN fold, at seed 42 and the representative 3×128 initialization, one deterministic first epoch-1 minibatch with valid FLP targets is probed. `autograd.grad` measures separate unweighted BP and FLP gradients on shared-backbone parameters, without optimizer steps. The **actual support** probe uses the same BP normalization and global FLP support correction as production training. The **common-valid support** probe restricts BP to FLP-valid rows and normalizes both tasks by that shared minibatch count. Both report gradient norms, BP/FLP norm ratio, dot product, and cosine similarity. The common-valid view diagnoses geometry on identical rows; it does not replace the production objective. The representative-architecture gradient probe is diagnostic and conditional on fixed architecture, initialization, data order, and hyperparameters.

## Bounded candidate and selection rule

Within each outer TRAIN population, the median across its five inner-fold probes of `||g_FLP|| / (||g_BP|| + ||g_FLP||)` proposes one weight. Four anchors `{0.1, 0.3, 0.7, 0.9}` and the historical `0.5` baseline give six candidates per outer study. The proposal is clipped to `[0.05, 0.95]`, rounded to four decimals, and deterministically moved at least 0.02 away from an anchor if necessary. The production CUDA BF16 probe, [saved with all 25 fold diagnostics](../../reports/training/07a/gradient_probe_cuda.json), proposes:

The earlier CPU FP32 probe is retained for provenance. Each outer-study balanced proposal differs from its production CUDA BF16 counterpart by less than 0.001; the CUDA proposals govern the planned sweep.

Every candidate uses seed 42, the same five frozen inner validation supports, and the representative 33-input 3×128 ReLU MLP with dropout zero, AdamW at learning rate 0.001 and weight decay 0.000316227766, batch size 65,536, and CUDA BF16. Every model runs to **100 complete epochs**; the rejected epoch-4 pruning strategy is not used. There are `5 outer × 6 alpha × 5 inner = 150` fold fits, or 15,000 fold-epochs. Each task persists per-epoch train/validation metrics, checkpoint, checksum, data and preprocessing provenance, and logs through the detached launcher. Interrupted tasks resume from their last complete checkpoint. Outer-study-3 `α=0.5` epoch 1 is checked against the accepted calibration reference.

At epoch 100, independently within each outer TRAIN, take the equal-fold mean of BP and FLP validation excess log losses for every alpha. Fold support must match across candidates. A point is empirically dominated when another has no larger objective in either task and is materially smaller in at least one (`1e−12` comparison tolerance). Among nondominated points, normalize each objective using the frontier minimum and span. Select the largest positive `1−x−y` bend when at least three frontier points and both spans are positive. Otherwise select the frontier point closest to the normalized ideal. Ties use smaller ideal distance, then proximity to `α=0.5`, then smaller alpha. This deterministic knee convention is a project-specific selection rule; no universal optimum is inferred from it. The resulting frontier and chosen alpha are conditional on the **fixed representative architecture and optimizer**. Figures, tables, fold metrics, and selection provenance are persisted. Outer TEST cannot influence alpha selection.

## Compute and execution boundary

The measured detached calibration took **247.41 s for 15 fold-epochs**, equivalent to 82.47 s per five-fold epoch at the observed two-worker schedule. The prior one-epoch training-only wall time was 80.92 s; full validation and checkpointing added work while later warm epochs had fewer cache misses. Across all 15 fold-epochs, explicit checkpoint save and load consumed 0.119 s and 0.064 s, respectively. The warm per-fold training-plus-validation range projects roughly **40–45 minutes per 100-epoch model**. Scaling the observed three-epoch wall time gives **2.29 hours per five-fold CV evaluation** and **68.7 hours for the 30-evaluation Pareto sweep**. These are projections, not measured 100-epoch runtimes. Plan approximately **75–100 hours** for fold and outer-population variation, cache behavior, process startup, failures, and artifact writing; retain the established two-worker, four-thread-per-worker resource gate. About 7.3 GB of checkpoints would accrue if the observed 487 KB per fold-epoch persists over 15,000 epochs. The observed 3-epoch window cannot quantify long-run checkpoint retention, storage pressure, or later-fold throughput exactly.

For later planning only, 150 unpruned five-fold HPO evaluations at the representative rate imply about **344 hours** before configuration variation and retries. The 06F epoch-4 pruning evidence does not support a lower expected budget. A later deep ensemble with `K` independently trained members per outer study requires `5K` final outer-TRAIN fits plus inference; those full-outer fits and `K` are not measured here. HPO and ensembles remain outside this checkpoint. No full Pareto sweep has been launched as part of this implementation review.
