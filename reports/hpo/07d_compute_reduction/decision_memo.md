# Phase 7D decision memo

**Recommendation:** After scientific approval, run **five successful, unrestricted, original-sampler configurations per remaining outer study** (1, 2, 4, 5), each on all five inner geographic folds for the full 100 epochs. Keep the frozen objective and the original outer TRAIN/TEST partitions. The sampler's ten-success startup rule means these five will be random startup suggestions; do not label this as TPE optimization. Stop at five successes per study, with failed attempts excluded from the count and retained in the audit. This memo does **not** authorize a launch or change the executable protocol.

The observed original-order pilot incumbent after five successes scored 0.104496, 0.003717 below the final best 0.108213. This single adaptive sequence cannot predict a fresh five-suggestion run. Fold reduction is not defensible: 2-, 3-, and 4-fold subsets matched the five-fold winner in 4/10, 6/10, and 4/5 cases, and every subset omitting fold 5 picked a different candidate. Epoch screening is not defensible: epoch-10 rank correlation with epoch 100 was −0.511, and epoch 75 still picked a different winner. Preserve five folds and 100 epochs.

At the pilot's 3.411 h per completed Optuna-trial interval, the recommended four-study HPO forecasts **68.22 serial trial-wall hours** for 100 fits, versus **272.88 h** for 400 fits under the original plan: **204.66 h, or 75%, less HPO time**. Four subsequent five-member full-TRAIN ensembles are separately estimated at **16.48 h of training** if their throughput resembles pilot trial 19; preprocessing, checkpoints, inference, retries, and regional runtime variation are extra. These are forecasts, not observed future costs. The Phase 7C local-runtime sum of 63.81 h excludes 4.41 h inside Optuna trial intervals; 68.22 h is the conservative scheduling basis.

**Scientific claims:** Each remaining study's eventual outer TEST is isolated from its own unrestricted inner selection. The untouched outer-3 TEST remains the principal confirmatory held-out evaluation of its original trial-19 selection. Because the *budget revision* was made after seeing outer-3 TRAIN validation data, which can overlap other studies' TEST populations, the pooled five-study result must be called a **revised-protocol geographic sensitivity summary**, not an unqualified, prospectively fixed unbiased nested-CV estimate. Do not use outer-test outcomes to revise this plan. Five random configurations may miss a strong region, and no practical equivalence margin has been established.

The [five exact pilot-informed configurations](proposed_pilot_informed_sweep.csv) are a **separate exploratory transfer sweep**, not the candidate restriction for the recommended independent searches. They are trial 19 (depth 3, width 64, batch 65536, LR 0.00011271913277710648, WD 0.0006325730720072149), trial 20 (3, 64, 65536, 0.00026946370940035357, 0.0005462595429734098), trial 6 (2, 64, 131072, 0.001217284708112243, 0.000026471141828218167), trial 8 (2, 64, 65536, 0.0004470608546778491, 0.0015446089075047066), and trial 4 (3, 128, 65536, 0.0021137059440645744, 0.00008612579192594876). Their purpose and observed runtimes are in the table. Reusing these candidates across other outer studies would introduce selection dependence; do not present the resulting tests as conventional independent nested CV.

**Approval gate:** Review and explicitly approve the revised five-success, five-fold, 100-epoch unrestricted protocol and its narrowed reporting claims **before** any study extension, training, ensemble refit, or outer TEST evaluation. The next experimental action after approval would be to update the launch protocol and execute **outer study 1 only** under that protocol, with a status check before proceeding to studies 2, 4, and 5. No training or protocol change is made in Phase 7D.
