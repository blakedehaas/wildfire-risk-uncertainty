# Phase 7C: completed outer-study-3 GradNorm HPO pilot

## Scope and reproducibility

This is a retrospective, read-only analysis of `wildfire_outer_3_gradnorm_hpo_v3`, with **20 COMPLETE** trials, **2 FAIL** attempts (1 and 17), and **100 final inner-fold fits**. Run `uv run python scripts/analyze_07c_outer3_hpo.py` from the repository root. The script opens the existing Optuna SQLite database with `mode=ro`, reads authoritative local epoch-100 records, and uses already published 6C geographic fold summaries and frozen reference losses in trial provenance. It writes only to this report directory. It checks each complete trial's configuration and objective against Optuna and recomputes both task skills from saved cross-entropies. Failed attempts remain in [the audit](trial_audit.csv), but contribute to no performance statistics. The study used a 100-epoch cap, no pruning, and 10 random startup completions followed by 10 TPE completions. Trial 10 belongs to startup because failed trial 1 did not satisfy TPE's startup count.

The frozen selection objective is the arithmetic mean across five inner geographic folds of `(BP skill + FLP skill)/2`, where task skill is `1 - validation model CE / validation constant-reference CE`. Each constant forecast was fitted on that fold's inner TRAIN support and scored on the original validation task mask. All findings here concern **inner validation**; no held-out outer TEST data or targets were inspected. [The prespecified rule](../../../docs/methodology/checkpoint_07b_hpo_objective.md) remains in force.

## Search trajectory and hyperparameters

Completed objective skill ranges **0.093959–0.108213** (median **0.102386**). The 10 random startup completions average **0.099469** (best **0.104496**); the 10 later TPE completions average **0.104232** (best **0.108213**). This is a descriptive comparison of sequential adaptive phases, not an unbiased sampler comparison. The incumbent improved at trials 11, 12, 15, and **19** after trial 0; the last two completed trials, 20 and 21, scored **0.106570** and **0.106199**. Their proximity to trial 19 suggests a promising local region. The improvement at trial 19 and lack of a long stable incumbent rule out a claim of demonstrated convergence or an asymptotic optimum. [Trajectory](figures/convergence.png) and [distribution](figures/distribution.png) show the full history.

The [hyperparameter slices](figures/hyperparameter_slices.png) show that all eight highest-ranking trials used depth 3 and width 64; the top three used batch size 65,536. Their learning rates were approximately **1.0–2.9 × 10⁻⁴**. These are observed promising configurations, not causal importance estimates. Only 20 points cover a five-dimensional search; TPE intentionally samples nonuniformly; depth, width, batch size, learning rate, and weight decay vary jointly. In particular, the observed average score of width 64 exceeds width 256, but the groups are small and differently composed. Weight decay spans several orders of magnitude among strong trials, so no narrow optimum is established.

The original per-task scores are fully recoverable from [all 100 final-fold records](trial_fold_metrics.csv). Trial 19's mean BP skill is **0.147134** and FLP skill is **0.069292**; its objective is their average. Across trials, fold 2 mean BP skill is slightly negative while FLP is slightly positive. Fold 5's high mean is driven especially by BP skill **0.435331**, alongside FLP **0.145264**. [Task balance](figures/task_balance.png) shows all configurations. Raw CE and fixed reference CE are retained in the fold table to prevent relative skill being mistaken for absolute calibration or forecast utility.

The frozen geographic report places folds 1–2 in western regions and folds 4–5 in eastern regions (EPSG:5070 centroids and support in [fold_summary.csv](fold_summary.csv)); fold membership was set by coordinates alone. Fold 2 has **17.98 million** validation rows and **15.01 million** FLP-valid rows; fold 5 has **14.44 million** and **8.58 million**, respectively. Fold 2's validation mean BP target is **0.004054** versus its TRAIN mean **0.002927**; fold 5's validation mean is **0.000132** versus TRAIN **0.003859**. This is a substantial target-distribution shift in opposite directions. The corresponding BP reference CEs, **0.02657** and **0.00460**, are highly different. Across completed trials, mean model BP CE is **0.02663** on fold 2 (slightly worse than its reference) and **0.00260** on fold 5 (much better than its reference). FLP shows a same-direction, smaller disparity: fold 2 model/reference CE **1.65533/1.67496**, fold 5 **1.19690/1.40032**. These arithmetic comparisons establish that the disparity is **not solely a BP denominator artifact**: fold 2 has little model improvement over its constant reference on both tasks. They do not isolate why the model generalizes differently. Regional target composition, predictor distributions, reference difficulty, and learned transfer may all contribute. A causal attribution is unsupported by the available aggregate artifacts.

The [TRAIN predictor missingness table](train_predictor_missingness.csv) recovers preprocessing counts, e.g. population-density missingness is **6.13%** for fold 2's TRAIN and **2.95%** for fold 5's TRAIN. These are training complements, not held-out regional predictor profiles. The current fold records do not contain validation predictor-coverage or missingness summaries. Consequently we cannot establish whether validation coverage explains fold 2's near-zero skill. The 6C frozen [spatial map](../../../spatial_validation/06c/figures/outer_3_inner_folds.png) gives geography without inspecting TEST targets.

## Selection and compute

Trial 19 (depth **3**, width **64**, batch **65,536**, learning rate **0.0001127191**, weight decay **0.0006325731**) is the highest observed original objective. Its lead over trial 20 is only **0.001643** skill, and over trial 21 **0.002014**. Because the maximum is selected from 20 noisy geographic scores, its observed lead is subject to selection optimism. There is no independent repeated-seed estimate, uncertainty interval for future geography, or outer-TEST result. The consistently weak fold 2 and the prior [high-risk BP adequacy finding](../../training/07b/README.md) remain scientific limitations; task-balanced HPO ranking does not repair calibration or high-risk prediction.

The 20 successful trials consumed **63.81 trial-runtime hours**, median **3.00 hours** each, excluding **8.63 hours** of failed-attempt wall time and any preparation/controller overhead. Median runtime by depth is **2.53 h** (depth 2, n=7) versus **3.85 h** (depth 3, n=13); by batch size **4.10 h** (32,768, n=10), **2.50 h** (65,536, n=8), and **2.07 h** (131,072, n=2). These are confounded observed groups, not isolated hardware scaling. [Runtime figure](figures/runtime.png) and [trial table](completed_trials.csv) retain exact values. Four more outer studies at this pilot's successful-trial runtime would require roughly **255 trial-runtime hours**; doubling to 40 successful trials would add roughly another **255 hours** at the same average cost, before failures or regional runtime changes. The late gain is real on this pilot's saved validation objective but too small and uncertain to quantify the expected return from a larger search.

## Interpretation and limitations

This single outer study cannot estimate the across-study distribution of optimal hyperparameters or geographic transfer. The five folds are fixed regions, not independent random replicates, and repeated trial scores share those regions. No p-values or confidence intervals are justified from treating 100 fits as 100 independent observations. TPE's adaptive proposals and the 10-startup threshold prevent a controlled random-versus-TPE effect estimate. The best trial lies near the lower learning-rate boundary; additional search might help, but the observed data cannot predict how much. No model training, checkpoint inference, modified objective, or outer TEST evaluation was used in this analysis.
