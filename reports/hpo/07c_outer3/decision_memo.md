# Phase 7C decision memo

**Recommendation: proceed with the frozen 20-successful-trial design for outer studies 1, 2, 4, and 5, subject to scientific review before launch.** This memo does not authorize training.

Trial 19 is the valid preferred configuration for outer study 3 under the prespecified equal-task, equal-fold objective. Its 0.108213 score leads the next two by only 0.001643 and 0.002014. A late improvement means convergence is unproven, but the last three trials cluster in a productive region; the pilot provides no quantitative basis for choosing a larger uniform budget. At about 63.81 successful-trial hours per study, the frozen four-study continuation already implies about 255 trial-runtime hours; doubling the budget would add a similar amount under the pilot's rate.

Fold 2 is worst and fold 5 best in all 20 configurations. Both task-specific skill and train/validation target shifts explain why the disparity demands interpretation; aggregate records do not identify a methodological defect or a causal source of weak transfer. Trial 6 improves the worst fold but loses 0.006237 in the frozen mean objective. Changing the ranking now would be retrospective. The scientific report should retain the geographic and task diagnostics, and final claims must await the untouched outer TEST evaluations under the frozen design.

If reviewers require stronger evidence of HPO convergence, a **prospectively specified uniform larger budget across all remaining outer studies** is the defensible alternative. The present pilot does not justify a particular larger count or expected gain. No issue demonstrated here requires changing training, splits, or objective implementation.
