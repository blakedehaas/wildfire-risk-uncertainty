# Checkpoint 6E: HPO orchestration and tracking validation

**Status:** HITL approved and frozen infrastructure validation. No scientific hyperparameter selection or outer TEST evaluation occurred. The real 30-trial × five-study HPO budget belongs to Checkpoint 6F and was not launched.

## Frozen orchestration

Each of the five outer studies has its own persistent SQLite Optuna study and authoritative local records under ignored `artifacts/hpo/`. The future 6F command requires an explicit outer study and authorization flag. Inside a study, one worker runs trials sequentially. The frozen [search space](search_space.json) uses `TPESampler(seed=42)` and `SuccessiveHalvingPruner(min_resource=1, reduction_factor=4, min_early_stopping_rate=0)` with a 100-epoch cap. Only depths 2/3, widths 64/128/256, logarithmic learning rate `[1e-4,1e-2]`, logarithmic AdamW weight decay `[1e-5,1e-2]`, and batch sizes 32768/65536/131072 vary. ReLU, dropout 0, BF16, 33 inputs, the BP/FLP losses, and equal task weights remain fixed.

The record hierarchy is **study → trial → inner fold → epoch**. A configuration is persisted in Optuna and an atomic local trial manifest before training. For each epoch, all five inner folds independently train and validate through the frozen 6D trainer and bounded-memory stream. Their raw loss sums and support counts are saved before online W&B logging. Each task mean is `loss_sum / support_count`; the fold objective is `0.5 × BP mean + 0.5 × FLP mean`. At a common epoch, (J_e) is the arithmetic mean of the five validation fold objectives. Geographic dispersion is reported as **population** standard deviation, minimum, and maximum. At the pruning rungs **1, 4, 16, 64**, and only after all five fold records exist, the orchestration reports (J_e) to Optuna and asks the pruner whether to continue. A survivor may reach epoch 100; epoch 100 is the fixed cap, not an additional pruning rung. Fold dispersion does not determine pruning.

The local atomic JSON records are authoritative for configuration, every training and validation raw sum/count/mean, task objective, timing, throughput, memory, step counts, pruning, state, and provenance. W&B is an online mirror with one run per trial; it cannot suggest configurations, prune, or alter the objective. Fold-specific `fold_K/epoch` metrics and `spatial_cv/resource_epoch` metrics define separate interactive chart axes. Local records precede W&B logging. If logging fails during a trial, the default marks sync **degraded/pending** and retains local calculations; a configurable abort mode exists. An incompatible persisted study metadata/search-space hash fails on resume. Recreated TPE samplers are **not** claimed to yield bitwise-identical future suggestions; the realized persisted trial history is the scientific record.
