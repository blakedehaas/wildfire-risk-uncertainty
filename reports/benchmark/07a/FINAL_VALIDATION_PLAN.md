Its five-fold epoch equivalent was 82.47 s with full validation and checkpoints; the conditional 100-epoch five-fold projection is 2.29 h. The later [Phase 7A protocol](../../../docs/methodology/checkpoint_07a_loss_balancing.md) supersedes the provisional budget below. All 15 checkpoint digests and in-run restores passed. The driver-update interruption occurred after this run; current PyTorch CUDA access was reverified before the gradient probe.

The prior detached training-only reference, `07a-20261020T183149Z-b5b9a54d`, completed one production epoch for all five inner folds of outer study 3 in **80.92 s wall time**, including subprocess startup and two-worker scheduling. Per-fold training took 20.52–27.02 s. This was longer than a warm 64-step extrapolation (about 59 s training for the same 318.1 million rows), demonstrating that short windows underestimated full-traversal cache and scheduling costs. The reference recorded no validation or checkpoints. Its peak sampled device VRAM was 3,131 MiB; this is a sample, not a sustained-memory bound.

The new detached calibration runs three consecutive epochs per fold, each with production `train_epoch` and full inner validation, on two processes with four CPU threads each. It loads the validated fold-specific preprocessing state, applies production preprocessing on every batch, and saves and reloads the provenance-bound production checkpoint after each epoch. It checks the restored model and optimizer hashes and epoch/global step. Epoch 1 training losses, supports, and optimizer steps must agree exactly with the reference. The production trainer checks finite logits, losses, gradients, and parameters; the benchmark additionally validates task loss arithmetic and frozen train/validation support. No outer TEST data or model selection is involved.

`resource_samples.csv` records device-wide GPU utilization and VRAM, worker RSS, process I/O bytes and major faults, guest available RAM, and swap every approximately one second. Per-epoch JSON files record training/validation time and throughput, checkpoint save/load times and hash, current RSS, CUDA allocation/reservation, I/O, and model/optimizer hashes. These measurements can expose cache warming and gross memory growth. WDDM GPU utilization is coarse and cannot alone prove GPU saturation. The supervisor stops on the established 15,500 MiB VRAM gate, less than 2 GiB guest RAM, or more than 128 MiB swap growth.

## Provisional computational arithmetic

Before the three-epoch result, the training-only reference implies **34–45 minutes of training** for one representative 100-epoch inner-fold model (20.52–27.02 s × 100), or **2.25 hours of training** for the five-fold outer-study-3 CV evaluation (80.92 s × 100). A six-weight, five-outer-study Pareto sweep contains 30 such CV evaluations, so its **training-only extrapolation is about 67.5 hours** if this outer study is representative. Full validation, checkpointing, startup, cache effects, and fold/outer variation must be added from the new run. The prior 64-step estimates of 9–13 hours for all 25 folds of one 100-epoch configuration remain optimistic until full validation is measured.

The frozen HPO plan has 30 trials per outer study, or 150 trials across five studies. Without pruning, its training-only extrapolation at this representative rate is **about 337 hours** (150 × 2.25 hours), before validation/checkpoints and configuration-dependent model cost. Epoch-4 pruning is known to be unreliable, so an idealized pruning lower bound is not a credible expected budget. Final deep-ensemble member count and retraining duration are not fixed in the inspected methodology; for `K` members trained separately in each of five outer studies, budget `5K` full outer-TRAIN model fits at the selected epoch count, plus inference and storage. Do not convert that formula to a single wall-clock promise without the member count and a measured full-outer fit rate.

## Readiness decision after detached completion

Phase 7A is ready for a Pareto launch only if all 15 fold-epoch records and full validation passes complete; epoch-1 training matches the frozen reference; BP/FLP supports and raw-loss arithmetic agree; all values and gradients are finite; every checkpoint checksum and restored state matches; there is no material epoch-to-epoch memory growth, swapping, or resource-gate event; and the observed five-fold epoch wall time supports a reviewed budget for 30 CV evaluations. If any condition fails, inspect the persisted worker logs and records before further training. No Pareto or HPO run is authorized by this benchmark launch.
